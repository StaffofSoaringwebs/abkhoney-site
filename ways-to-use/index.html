Five ways to use raw honey that aren’t toast.

Red gum is a eucalyptus honey. As a variety it runs darker and more savoury than a light clover honey, so it holds its own beside salt, cheese and heat.

Five

  1. Porridge, after the pan comes off

    Stir it in off the heat rather than while it is boiling. Long heat flattens the flavour of any honey — there is no reason to pay for character and then cook it out.

  2. Over something sharp on a cheese board

    A blue, or a hard cheddar. Spoon it on and let it run down the side. A savoury honey against a salty cheese is the whole trick.

  3. A glaze for roast vegetables or chicken

    Honey, olive oil, a squeeze of lemon, salt. Brush it on in the last ten minutes of roasting so the sugar colours instead of catching.

  4. Salad dressing, by the jar

    One part honey, two parts vinegar, three parts oil, a pinch of salt. Shake it in a jar with the lid on — it emulsifies without a whisk.

  5. Hot lemon at the end of the day

    Lemon, a spoon of honey, water just off the boil. We are making no claim about what that does for you. It tastes good on a cold night, which is reason enough.
